\# Refund Policy



\*\*Last Updated: January 2026\*\*



\## Our Commitment



At Huria Delivery, customer satisfaction is our priority, we strive to ensure your satisfaction with every purchase. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, we're here to help.





\## Returns Window



You have \*\*7 calendar days\*\* from the date of delivery to request a return.



\## Eligibility Criteria



To be eligible for a return:



\- Item must be unused and in original condition that you received it

\- Original packaging must be intact

\- Proof of purchase is required

\- Items must not be damaged by customer



\## Non-Returnable Items



The following cannot be returned:

\- Perishable goods

\- Personal care items

\- Gift cards

\- Downloadable software

\- Custom products



\## Refund Process



1\. Contact our support team within 7 days

2\. Provide order number and reason for return

3\. Our team will approve and provide return instructions

4\. Ship the item back (customer pays return shipping unless item is defective)

5\. Refund processed within 5-7 business days after inspection

6\. Once we receive your item, we will inspect it and notify you on the status of your refund. If approved, we will initiate a refund to your original method of payment.



\## Refund Method



Refunds are issued to the original payment method:

\- Mobile Money: 24-48 hours

\- Credit/Debit Card: 5-7 business days

\- Bank Transfer: 7-10 business days
