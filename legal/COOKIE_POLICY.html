Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website or use our app.

3. Cookie Management

3.1 Browser Settings

You can control cookies through your browser:

Browser How to Manage
Chrome Settings → Privacy → Cookies
Firefox Options → Privacy → Cookies
Safari Preferences → Privacy
Edge Settings → Cookies

3.2 Mobile App

  • Mobile apps use device identifiers
  • Can reset in device settings

3.3 Opt-Out

  • Essential cookies cannot be disabled
  • Analytics and marketing cookies can be opted out
  • Cookie consent banner available on first visit
