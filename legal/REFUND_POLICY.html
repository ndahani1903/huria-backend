REFUND POLICY - HURIA DELIVERY

Effective Date: 01/05/2026

Last Updated:

1. Our Commitment

At Huria Delivery, customer satisfaction is our priority, we strive to ensure your satisfaction with every purchase. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, we're here to help.

2. Returns Window

You have 7 calendar days from the date of delivery to request a return.

3. Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for a return:

  • Item must be unused and in original condition that you received it
  • Original packaging must be intact
  • Proof of purchase is required
  • Items must not be damaged by customer

4. Non-Returnable Items

The following cannot be returned:

  • Perishable goods
  • Personal care items
  • Gift cards
  • Downloadable software
  • Custom products

5. Refund Process

  • Contact our support team within 7 days
  • Provide order number and reason for return
  • Our team will approve and provide return instructions
  • Ship the item back (customer pays return shipping unless item is defective)
  • Refund processed within 5-7 business days after inspection
  • Once we receive your item, we will inspect it and notify you on the status of your refund. If approved, we will initiate a refund to your original method of payment.

6. Refund Method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method:

  • Mobile Money: 24-48 hours
  • Credit/Debit Card: 5-7 business days
  • Bank Transfer: 7-10 business days

7. Contact Us

For any questions about our refund policy, please contact our support team.

  • For return requests: support@huria.com
  • Phone: +255 624 729 596
