\# COOKIE POLICY - HURIA DELIVERY



\*\*Effective Date:\*\* 1/5/2026



\## 1. What Are Cookies?



Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website or use our app.



\## 2. Cookies We Use



\### 2.1 Essential Cookies (Required)



| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |

|--------|---------|----------|

| session\_id | Keep you logged in | Session |

| csrf\_token | Security protection | Session |

| cart\_id | Maintain shopping cart | 30 days |



\### 2.2 Functional Cookies



| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |

|--------|---------|----------|

| language | Remember language preference | 1 year |

| location | Save delivery address | 90 days |

| theme | UI preferences | 1 year |



\### 2.3 Analytics Cookies



| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |

|--------|---------|----------|

| \_ga | Google Analytics | 2 years |

| \_gid | Google Analytics | 24 hours |

| \_gat | Throttle requests | 1 minute |



\### 2.4 Marketing Cookies



| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |

|--------|---------|----------|

| \_fbp | Facebook tracking | 90 days |

\## 3. Cookie Management



\### 3.1 Browser Settings



You can control cookies through your browser:



| Browser | How to Manage |

|---------|---------------|

| Chrome | Settings → Privacy → Cookies |

| Firefox | Options → Privacy → Cookies |

| Safari | Preferences → Privacy |

| Edge | Settings → Cookies |



\### 3.2 Mobile App



\- Mobile apps use device identifiers

\- Can reset in device settings



\### 3.3 Opt-Out



\- Essential cookies cannot be disabled

\- Analytics and marketing cookies can be opted out

\- Cookie consent banner available on first visit
