\# INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT - HURIA DELIVERY



\*\*Effective Date:\*\* \[DATE]



\## 1. Independent Contractor Status



This is an \*\*independent contractor agreement\*\*, NOT an employment contract.



| Aspect | Status |

|--------|--------|

| Employment | ❌ NOT employee |

| Benefits | ❌ No health, leave, pension |

| Taxes | ✅ Self-employed (pay own taxes) |

| Equipment | ✅ Own smartphone, vehicle |

| Schedule | ✅ Choose when to work |



\## 2. Services



Driver agrees to:

\- Accept delivery requests via app

\- Pick up orders from merchants

\- Deliver to customer locations

\- Maintain professional conduct

\- Follow traffic laws



\## 3. Earnings



\### 3.1 Delivery Fees



| Component | Amount |

|-----------|--------|

| Base fee | 2,000 TSh |

| Distance (per km) | 500 TSh |

| Waiting time (>10 min) | 100 TSh/min |

| Peak hour (5-8pm) | +30% |

| Night shift (10pm-5am) | +50% |

| Rain/special conditions | +20% |



\### 3.2 Example Calculation

Distance: 5 km = 2,500 TSh

Base fee: 2,000 TSh

Total before bonuses: 4,500 TSh

Peak hour (+30%): 1,350 TSh

Customer tip: 1,000 TSh

TOTAL: 6,850 TSh



\### 3.3 Payment Schedule



| Payout Type | Schedule |

|-------------|----------|

| Daily | Request after 50,000 TSh |

| Weekly | Every Monday |

| Minimum payout | 20,000 TSh |



\## 4. Driver Requirements



\### 4.1 Documentation



| Document | Requirement |

|----------|-------------|

| Driver license | Valid, Class C or higher |

| Vehicle registration | Current |

| Insurance | Third-party minimum |

| NIDA ID | Valid |

| TIN (Tax ID) | For payments >500,000 TSh/month |

| Background check | Clean record |



\### 4.2 Vehicle Requirements



| Vehicle Type | Requirements |

|--------------|--------------|

| Motorcycle | 150cc+, helmet, safety gear |

| Car | 4-door, A/C optional |

| Truck | Payload capacity, cargo cover |



\### 4.3 Quality Standards



| Standard | Requirement |

|----------|-------------|

| Acceptance rate | >70% |

| Completion rate | >95% |

| Customer rating | >4.5 |

| On-time delivery | >90% |



\## 5. Bonuses and Incentives



| Bonus | Requirement | Amount |

|-------|-------------|--------|

| Completion bonus | 50 deliveries/week | 20,000 TSh |

| Perfect week | 100% completion, >4.8 rating | 50,000 TSh |

| Rating bonus | >4.9 rating (week) | 30,000 TSh |

| Referral | New driver completes 100 deliveries | 50,000 TSh |

| Streak | 30 days active | 100,000 TSh |



\## 6. Deductions and Penalties



| Violation | Penalty |

|-----------|---------|

| Late pickup (>15 min) | -20% of delivery fee |

| Wrong delivery | -100% fee + compensation |

| Rude behavior | Suspension + retraining |

| Fraudulent activity | Termination + legal action |

| Safety violation | Suspension |



\## 7. Safety Requirements



\- Follow all traffic laws

\- Wear safety gear (motorcycle)

\- Do not use phone while driving

\- Report accidents immediately

\- Maintain vehicle properly

\- No alcohol/substances



\## 8. Insurance



| Type | Coverage | Provided By |

|------|----------|-------------|

| Third-party | Legal minimum | Driver's own |

| Accident | Medical expenses | Optional |

| Theft | Equipment | Driver's own |

| Public liability | Third-party claims | Huria (during active deliveries) |



\## 9. Conduct Standards



Driver must NOT:

\- Harass customers or merchants

\- Accept cash payments (platform only)

\- Share customer information

\- Use customer data for marketing

\- Tamper with deliveries



\## 10. Termination



\### 10.1 By Driver

\- Delete app or notify support

\- Final payout within 14 days



\### 10.2 By Huria



Immediate termination for:

\- Criminal activity

\- Customer complaints (legitimate)

\- Fraud

\- Safety violations

\- False information



\## 11. Tax Obligations



\- Driver responsible for own taxes

\- Withholding tax (5%) deducted from earnings

\- TIN required for annual earnings >2M TSh

\- Annual filing required



\## 12. Equipment



\- Driver provides smartphone (Android 10+ or iOS 14+)

\- Data costs borne by driver

\- Huria provides delivery bags (refundable deposit: 50,000 TSh)



\## 13. Independent Contractor Acknowledgment



Driver acknowledges:

\- Not an employee of Huria

\- No guaranteed earnings

\- No fixed schedule

\- No statutory benefits

\- Can work for competitors

\- Pays own taxes



\## 14. Dispute Resolution



1\. Contact driver support: drivers@huria.com

2\. Internal review within 48 hours

3\. Mediation if unresolved

4\. Arbitration for disputes >500,000 TSh
