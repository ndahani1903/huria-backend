\## 6. Your Rights



Under Tanzanian Data Protection Law, you have:



| Right | Description | How to Exercise |

|-------|-------------|-----------------|

| Right to access | Know what data we hold | Request via email |

| Right to rectification | Correct inaccurate data | Update in profile |

| Right to deletion | Remove your data | Delete account |

| Right to restriction | Limit processing | Contact DPO |

| Right to portability | Receive data copy | Request export |

| Right to object | Stop processing | Opt-out |

| Right to withdraw consent | Stop marketing | Unsubscribe |



\*\*Response time:\*\* Within 30 days of request

If a breach occurs:

5\. Remediation plan implemented
